Appendix · A6

Definitions used in this deck

ARR = subscription MRR after discounts at 31 Oct 2026 × 12. Annual plans count 1/12 per month. Excludes one-time fees and usage-tier fees ($732k run-rate, shown separately).

Paying customer = billed above $0 for October service. Trials never count. Brightline (an agency billing for many brands) counts as one customer.

Growth = MRR Oct 2026 ÷ MRR Apr 2026, both from invoices by service month, never cash.

Gross margin = (subscription + usage revenue − LLM inference − hosting − support tools) ÷ revenue, from bank payments.

CAC = bank marketing spend by channel ÷ new paying customers by Stripe channel tag, May–Oct 2026. Platform-reported conversions are never used.

Runway = bank cash at 31 Oct 2026 ÷ average net burn of Aug–Oct 2026.

Source: calc/metrics.csv and out/calc_table.csv in the source repository list the formula and source for every number
